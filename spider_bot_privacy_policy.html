Spider-Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how the Spider-Bot project may handle information when you use its voice commands, Discord features, or connected-device controls.

Owner: Please review before publishing. This is a starter template. Update it so it accurately describes the code and services you actually use. Remove any feature that Spider-Bot does not have.

Information Spider-Bot may process

  • Voice commands: If voice recognition is enabled, Spider-Bot processes audio or recognized text to understand commands. If you configure offline speech recognition, processing may happen on your computer. Any online speech service you add may receive audio or text according to that service's policies.
  • Discord: If you use the Discord bot, it processes the Discord messages and commands needed to respond or carry out an action. Discord also processes information under its own policies.
  • Device commands: If connected-device control is enabled, Spider-Bot processes the command needed to control the supported device.
  • Logs: The program or its libraries may create local logs or error messages. Review your code and settings to determine what is saved and for how long.

How information is used

Information is used to recognize commands, respond in the configured Discord server, troubleshoot errors, and perform features that the user has enabled.

Sharing and third-party services

Spider-Bot should only send information to services needed for enabled features. For example, Discord messages are handled by Discord. Other services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Do not enter passwords, bot tokens, or other secrets into chat messages or public channels.

Storage and security

Keep bot tokens and API keys private. Store them outside public source code, such as in environment variables or a local .env file that is excluded from version control. Anyone who obtains a bot token may be able to misuse the bot; reset a token if it is exposed. Actual storage and security depend on the project's implementation and your computer's settings.

Voice controls

Use a clear indicator or command to show when voice listening is active, and provide a way to stop listening. Do not record or process other people's conversations without their knowledge and appropriate permission.

Children and personal information

Do not use Spider-Bot to collect sensitive personal information. A parent or trusted adult should review online accounts, permissions, and connected services when appropriate.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Spider-Bot changes. Update the date above when you make a meaningful change.
